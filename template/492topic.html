Project Description:

Dynamic mechanical analyzer (DMA) is an accurate experimental system suited towards the determination of dynamic mechanical behaviour of soft tissues. Muscles consist of soft tissues surrounding fibers that enable motion of the limbs by contracting. It is important to conduct various tests in order to shed light onto the complex mechanical behaviour of these type of tissues. The test results will then be numerically processed to estimate muscles material parameters. This work is part of a TUBITAK project and will be supervised by a graduate student.
