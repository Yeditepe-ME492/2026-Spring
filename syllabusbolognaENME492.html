Goals

To equip students with the ability to conduct a project under the supervision of a faculty member, but with much more personal effort and personal initiative than in a lecture-type course. The course may be conducted one-on-one or, at times, as a team of two students working on the same project under the same faculty member, in the latter case of which the contribution of each student must be clearly defined by the advisor.

Content

Conducting a project related to analysis or design of a mechanical engineering system, simulation of a physical/engineering phenomenon, conducting a series of experiments to prove/disprove a concept/idea, and the like.

Contribution of the Course to Engineering Education

Students gain self confidence that they can work on a project with minimal supervision, which in the future may lead them to undertake projects fully on their own.
